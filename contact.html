BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few things to keep in mind.

To ensure a smooth and enjoyable beauty experience, please take a moment to review our booking policies and service requirements.

01

Booking Payment

Clients are required to pay 85% of the service fee upfront. Payment is required to validate and secure an appointment.

02

Non-Refundable Payments

All payments made toward a booking are non-refundable. Please ensure your booking details are correct before making payment.

03

Home Service Environment

Clients must provide a well-secured and conducive environment for makeup application. The room should accommodate no more than two people.

04

Timeliness

Clients are expected to be ready before the makeup artist arrives. A ₦10,000 penalty applies if the client is not ready upon arrival.

05

Home Service Charges

Home service charges depend on the client's location. The farther the location, the higher the applicable service charge.

06

Booking Confirmation

Your appointment is confirmed once the required booking payment has been received and acknowledged by the makeup artist.
